## 📌 Backlog / Planned Future Refinements

### 🗓️ Date Picker & Custom Date Range UX Overhaul
- **Task**: Redesign the Custom Date Range selection panel & Date Keypad Modal.
- **User Feedback**: Current custom date range inputs, popovers, and touch keypad feel clunky/un-intuitive and need a fresh ground-up UX redesign.
- **Planned Refinements**:
  1. Replace native date inputs with a dedicated custom dual-calendar visual range picker popover (similar to modern analytics dashboards like Airbnb/Stripe).
  2. Redesign the touch keypad modal with a smoother inline date roller / wheel picker or visual calendar grid touch target.
  3. Simplify date range presets bar into a floating popover selector to reduce vertical space usage.
